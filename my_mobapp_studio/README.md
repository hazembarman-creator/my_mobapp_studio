# Welcome to My Mobapp Studio
***

## Task
The goal of this project is to analyze the Google Play Store market to help My MobApp Studio decide which category of mobile app to build next.
The challenge lies in:

Working with a messy real‑world dataset (missing values, inconsistent formats, duplicates).

Extracting business‑ready insights from raw data.

Producing both a technical notebook and a scientific-style blog report.

Generating visualizations that marketing and design teams can understand at a glance.

The goal of this project is to analyze the Google Play Store market to help My MobApp Studio decide which category of mobile app to build next.
The challenge lies in:

Working with a messy real‑world dataset (missing values, inconsistent formats, duplicates).

Extracting business‑ready insights from raw data.

Producing both a technical notebook and a scientific-style blog report.

Generating visualizations that marketing and design teams can understand at a glance.


## Description
This project solves the problem by:

Loading and cleaning the Google Play Store dataset.

Implementing reusable analysis functions:

load_dataset()

print_summarize_dataset()

clean_dataset()

print_histograms()

compute_correlations_matrix()

print_scatter_matrix()

Computing:

Market size (downloads + estimated revenue)

Category‑level percentages

Downloads‑per‑app ratios

Producing all required visualizations:

Bar chart of most popular paid Family apps

Pie chart of genres within paid Family apps

Installations per category (array + pie chart)

Mean price per category (bar chart)

Most expensive apps per category

Writing a scientific blog post summarizing assumptions, methodology, results, and next steps.

The final output includes:

A complete Jupyter Notebook with code and plots

A blog post published online (URL stored in link_to_blog_post.txt)
